Which guardrails for health care AI have evidence behind them?

Frameworks, statutes, and commentaries recommend many practices for deploying AI with patients and staff, from large language models and ambient scribes to predictive risk models, imaging software, and agents. This map places each recommended practice by how many sources endorse it and by the strongest study that has tested it, and it flags whether any test included Medicaid, safety-net, limited English proficiency, or low-literacy populations.
